ABOUT THE GROUP

SEVEN MEMBERS.
ONE ENHYPEN.

ENHYPEN is a seven-member South Korean boy group under BELIFT LAB. The members are Heeseung, Jay, Jake, Sunghoon, Sunoo, Jungwon and Ni-Ki.

The group was formed through the survival program I-LAND and officially debuted on November 30, 2020. Their name represents the connection between individuals, just like a hyphen connects words.

Their music and concepts often explore themes of youth, connection, desire, identity and the boundaries between different worlds.
